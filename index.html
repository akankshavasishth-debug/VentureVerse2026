About the Event

VentureVerse 2026 is a one-day, student-focused event about turning early ideas into real ventures. Hear honest startup journeys, try a hands-on idea sprint, and discover how entrepreneurship can start right here on campus.

💡

Learn from Founders

Listen to real stories of struggles, pivots and wins from people who built startups from scratch.

⚡

Hands-on Idea Sprint

Team up with other students and shape a campus idea into a simple plan in just 75 minutes.

🤝

Meet and Network

Connect with like-minded students, mentors and speakers who can help your ideas grow.

Event Schedule

Here is how the day will flow, from welcome to networking.

  1. 10:00 AM

    Welcome

    Opening remarks and introduction to VentureVerse 2026.

  2. 10:20 AM

    From Idea to Startup

    A talk on how a small idea becomes a real, working venture.

  3. 11:15 AM

    Founder Story

    A founder shares their journey, mistakes and lessons learned.

  4. 12:00 PM

    Campus Idea Sprint

    Teams brainstorm and build a quick plan for a campus problem.

  5. 1:15 PM

    Pitching Basics

    Learn how to explain your idea clearly and confidently in a few minutes.

  6. 2:15 PM

    Networking and Closing

    Meet speakers and fellow students, then close the day together.
